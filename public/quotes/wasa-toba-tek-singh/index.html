Executive summary

Begin with the two capabilities WASA needs in operation now.

Phase 1 brings existing billing data and public complaints into a structured digital workflow quickly and at a fixed cost. It is deliberately scoped as an operational starting point — and it is built on an architecture that later phases extend rather than replace.

Initial Billing Management

Billing data imported and structured. Consumers retrieve and print a duplicate bill from the WASA website.

Complaint Management Foundation

Complaints stored permanently, tracked through a defined status workflow, with a full history against every case.

Operational Dashboard

A single view of complaint volume, status and category, with filtering and CSV export for the Complaint Cell.

Initial Billing Management

Existing billing data is brought into a structured digital workflow, and consumers can retrieve and print their bill from the WASA website without visiting an office.

CSV billing data import

  • Upload billing data through CSV
  • Structured field validation
  • Consumer record mapping

Consumer bill retrieval

  • Search by consumer ID or connection number

Phase 1 · Included In scope now

Complaint Management Foundation

The website complaint form is connected to a persistent backend workflow. A complaint becomes a tracked record with an owner, a status and a history — not a temporary notification.

Public side

  • Complaint submission form
  • Contact information
  • Complaint category
  • Description and location details
  • Complaint reference number
  • Submission confirmation

Administrative side

  • View, search and filter complaints
  • Update complaint status
  • Assign and track responsible person
  • Add internal notes
  • Record resolution information
  • Full complaint history
  • Monitor pending and resolved cases
  • Flag cases needing follow-up

Case status workflow

NewLogged with reference number
Under reviewValidated and categorised
AssignedResponsible person recorded
Field actionSite visit or intervention
ResolvedResolution recorded
ClosedCase completed

Referred or not resolved

Where a case cannot be closed, staff record the reason so nothing disappears silently:

Location not accessible Consumer unavailable Issue could not be verified Field action pending Belongs to another department Requires further investigation Other — recorded reason

Traceable history

Every case retains its full trail, giving management accountability and traceability:

Submitted → Assigned → Status changed → Field action →
Resolution attempt → Reason recorded if unresolved →
Referred or reopened → Final resolution → Closure
